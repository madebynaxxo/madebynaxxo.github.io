YAHBA — Water Ritual

Freelance — 2026

A skincare brand that crosses a Mayan soul with the Korean method. Cenote water, filtered for thousands of years through sascab limestone, gives both name and substance to a five-step ritual named after real Mayan words: SAK, Kuxtal, Yaax, K'in. Full identity manual — logotype, palette, typography and a reef-safe packaging system — with the blue mangrove crab as the brand's guardian. The whole case can be walked through as a storefront built on top of the identity.

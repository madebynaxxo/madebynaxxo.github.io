Academic profile

Jaime Iñaki Arroyo Domínguez

Graduate in Advertising and Public Relations

My education is split across three countries and three university systems. I started at UPAEP (Puebla, Mexico) on a merit scholarship won back in high school, spent a year at Keimyung University (Daegu, South Korea) on the Korean government's GKS scholarship, and finished the degree at the University of Murcia.

That path is also what my research is about: how visual communication builds collective memory, comparing two movements that happened twelve years and ten thousand kilometres apart.

03 — Research

Final dissertation

University of Murcia · 2026 · Grade: First Class 9.0/10

Final dissertation University of Murcia · 2026
Persuasive communication and social advertising as tools of collective memory

A comparative and ethical analysis of how visual communication and social advertising build collective memory around two student resistance movements: Tlatelolco (Mexico, 1968) and Gwangju (South Korea, 1980). Two countries I know from the inside, two massacres twelve years apart, and the question of how images decide what gets remembered and what gets forgotten.
